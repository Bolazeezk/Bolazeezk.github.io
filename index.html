About Me

I am a mathematics graduate with research interests in time series analysis, mathematical modeling, statistical methods, and filtering and smoothing techniques.

My master's research at Illinois State University examined polynomial-preserving linear filters and their applications to smoothing noisy time series while preserving underlying polynomial trends.

Research Interests

  • Mathematical and statistical modeling
  • Polynomial-preserving linear filters
  • Filtering, smoothing, and trend estimation
  • Numerical methods and computational mathematics

Master's Thesis Research

My thesis presents a unified mathematical treatment of polynomial-preserving smoothing filters, including Savitzky–Golay filters and the Spencer 15-point moving average.

The work examines polynomial reproduction through moment conditions, provides explicit derivations of classical filter coefficients, and uses numerical experiments to compare smoothing performance.
